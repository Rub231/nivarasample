Treatments / Anti-Ageing

Conservative Botox & Expression Softening

Softens frown lines, forehead lines and crow's feet while keeping natural expression. Administered with conservative dosing and an automatic 14-day review.

The "Look Rested, Not Frozen" Philosophy

The fear most patients have regarding anti-wrinkle injections is looking stiff, emotionless, or unnaturally tight. At Nivara, Dr. Ananya Mehrotra uses micro-dosing and targeted anatomical placement to preserve expressive micro-movements.

Your brows will still raise, you will still laugh naturally, but the persistent hyperactive muscle tension that creases your skin will be gently calmed.

Primary Treatment Areas

Glabellar Lines

The "11" vertical frown lines between the brows caused by squinting or concentration.

Horizontal Forehead

Subtly softened while ensuring the brows remain lifted, not heavy or droopy.

Crow's Feet

Fine lines radiating around the outer eyes, softened while retaining warm smile crinkles.

Standard Practice

Mandatory 14-Day Checkup

Botox takes 10 to 14 days to fully stabilize within neuromuscular junctions. We schedule every patient for a 14-day in-clinic review with Dr. Mehrotra to evaluate symmetry, movement, and make micro-touchups if necessary.

Contraindications & Safety:

• Not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
• Not suitable for patients with certain neuromuscular conditions (e.g. Myasthenia Gravis).
• Placement is strictly performed by Dr. Ananya Mehrotra (never delegated to nursing or technician staff).
