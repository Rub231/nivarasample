Clinical Philosophy

"You should look rested, not done. My job is to tell you what will help, what won't, and what can wait."

Aesthetic medicine has too often become transactional. Patients walk into clinics and are presented with rigid packages, aggressive upselling, and rushed treatments performed by technicians without clinical oversight.

At Nivara, Dr. Mehrotra practices under a simple rule: Diagnose first, treat second. She sees a strictly limited number of patients each day so there is genuine time to talk, examine skin in clinic lighting, understand previous treatments, and build a measured, step-by-step plan.

Our Core Promise: We tell you what you don't need.

If a treatment isn't indicated for your skin or if a procedure will create an artificial or overdone appearance, Dr. Mehrotra will advise against it. Restraint is the highest form of aesthetic skill.
