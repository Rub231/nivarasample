Treatments / Pigmentation & Melasma

Pigmentation & Melasma Protocol

Treated in layers: topical care, oral medicine if needed, and gentle peels or laser toning. Designed to manage pigment without triggering rebound hyperpigmentation.

Why generic treatments often fail

Melasma is a chronic, vascular-and-pigmentary condition influenced by hormones, UV light, and heat. Aggressive lasers or harsh parlor peels generate excess inflammation, which causes melanocytes to rebound and darken the patches further.

At Nivara, Dr. Ananya Mehrotra approaches pigmentation medically: stabilizing the skin barrier first, calming active inflammation, and gently fragmenting melanin using calibrated laser toning and medical peeling agents.

The 3-Phase Protocol

Phase 01 · Medical Stabilization

Prescription topical tyrosinase inhibitors, barrier repair ceramides, and oral therapy (such as low-dose tranexamic acid, if medically indicated).

Phase 02 · In-Clinic Gentle Interventions

3 to 5 sessions of targeted medical chemical peels or USFDA low-fluence laser toning. Spaced 2 to 3 weeks apart.

Phase 03 · Relapse Prevention

Ongoing photoprotection protocols (tinted mineral zinc oxide sunscreens) to block both UV and visible blue light.

Important Candidacy Note:

• Minimal downtime with mild dryness.
• Not suitable if the patient cannot avoid direct sun for two weeks after a procedure.
• Pregnancy: Certain oral or strong peeling agents are paused during pregnancy and breastfeeding.
