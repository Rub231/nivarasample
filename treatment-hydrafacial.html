HydraFacial MD

A doctor-supervised cleanse, vacuum vortex exfoliation, and targeted serum hydration facial. 45 minutes, zero downtime.

The Medical Difference

Unlike salon facials that use abrasive scrubs or manual comedone extractions that pinch and scar the skin, HydraFacial MD uses patented Vortex-Fusion technology.

At Nivara, suction levels, glycolic/salicylic peeling tips, and antioxidant infusion boosters are individually selected by Dr. Mehrotra according to your skin’s barrier health.

The 3 In-Clinic Steps

Step 01 · Cleanse + Peel

Gentle exfoliation with lactic and glycolic solutions uncovers a fresh layer of skin without scratching or micro-tearing.

Step 02 · Extract + Hydrate

Painless vacuum vortex suction clears blackheads, sebum, and congestion from pores while saturating skin with intense moisturizers.

Step 03 · Fuse + Protect

Infusion of hyaluronic acid, peptides, and antioxidant complexes to maximize hydration and restore skin luminosity.

Contraindications & Maintenance:

• Repeated monthly for ongoing skin barrier resilience and congestion control.
• Not suitable over active, inflamed cystic infections, fresh sunburn, or immediately post-resurfacing laser.
